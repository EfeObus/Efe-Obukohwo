Nashville Housing Market Analysis

Advanced Excel Business Intelligence & Market Analytics

2013 – 2016 Data Data Analyst Portfolio Project 56,636 Raw Records

Project Overview

Raw, unstructured municipal data often obscures critical commercial realities. This project focused on engineering a messy dataset of 56,636 rows into a robust, stakeholder-ready business intelligence solution using advanced Excel. By architecting a single, verified master data model and layering a dynamic formula engine, this project uncovered core macro trends, transaction seasonality, and geographical value distributions across the Nashville housing market.

The Business Problem

In its raw state, the public municipal housing dataset was unusable for strategic decision-making due to duplicate records, incomplete property appraisals, unformatted data features, and a lack of comparative metrics. Stakeholders required a clean database paired with an analytical framework to easily see where capital was being deployed and identify changing market trajectories over a four-year period.

Vision

Turn a 56,636-row public extract riddled with duplicates and unformatted fields into a single verified master data model — and a formula engine that lets stakeholders slice it by year, city, and price tier without touching a line of code.

Methodology & Technical Execution

Data Quality and Data Engineering

  • De-duplication: Conducted algorithmic checks to flag and eliminate 168 exact-duplicate sales records across multiple spatial keys.
  • Defensive Null Value Mapping: Designed a formula-driven routing system that categorized 30,619 unvalued records as "N/A" instead of converting them to zero, preventing structural skews in downstream market-tier analysis.
  • Feature Isolation: Programmed derived calculation columns to parse time dimensions (SaleYear, SaleMonth) and standardized boolean flags.

Advanced Analytics Engine

  • Dynamic Record Retrieval: Leveraged VLOOKUP for boundary-based market tiering, alongside complex XLOOKUP and INDEX/MATCH lookup systems to associate property records cleanly.
  • Aggregated Micro-Insights: Constructed localized statistical lookups utilizing multi-criteria conditional filters including SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, and AVERAGEIFS.
  • Filter-Aware Architecture: Replaced standard aggregation cells with custom SUBTOTAL and SUMPRODUCT logic arrays, allowing the workbook's metrics to safely recalculate in real time without dropping context.

Business Intelligence Dashboard

  • Constructed nine granular pivot tables evaluating rolling totals, localized price variations, and operational market distributions.
  • Built a custom front-end dashboard in Excel containing five main KPI indicators and a 2x3 interactive matrix chart layout, fully responsive to dynamic user slicers.

Capital Share — Nashville Proper
  • Aggregated Market Value: The dataset captured an overall market transaction profile totaling $18.50 billion across 56,468 verified single-record sales.
  • The 2015 Peak: Discovered an aggressive growth phase where market volume climbed by 52.0% in dollar investment from 2013 to a distinct regional peak of $6.71 billion in 2015, before softening slightly in 2016.
  • Geographic Concentration: Uncovered a heavy real estate centralization pattern where Nashville proper accounted for $14.73 billion — representing roughly 80% of all capital movement in the territory.
  • Volume Distribution: Pinpointed that the regional market is built heavily on mid-market purchasing power, with 32,826 total transactions falling within the $100,000 to $300,000 threshold.

Strategic Impact & Recommendations

Geographical Resource Allocation

Recommended that investors prioritize inventory and marketing capital toward Nashville proper and Antioch, as these two sub-markets anchor the vast majority of regional liquidity.

Seasonal Launch Strategy

Advised stakeholders to time property listings for late spring to directly capture the peak purchasing season that historically peaks in June.

Risk Mitigation

Highlighted the volume and value cooling observed in 2016 as a key leading indicator to monitor closely before engaging in further unhedged long-term capital acquisitions.
