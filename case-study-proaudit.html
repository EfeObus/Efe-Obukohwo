The Problem

Nigeria's 2026 tax reforms introduced sweeping changes that caught most businesses unprepared:

  • Mandatory NRS e-invoicing – All businesses must generate electronic invoices through the new National Revenue Service system
  • Progressive PAYE with ₦800,000 tax-free threshold – Complex salary calculations with new bands and reliefs
  • Input VAT recovery on services and assets – Tracking requirements for VAT credits
  • Zero-CIT eligibility for small businesses – Qualification criteria and documentation requirements
  • New development levies and Minimum Effective Tax Rate (ETR) – Additional compliance layers

The Gap

Most existing accounting tools were built for other jurisdictions. Nigerian businesses were forced to rely on error-prone spreadsheets and manual processes, exposing them to penalties, audit failures, and fraud risks.

Technical Decisions

Why FastAPI over Django/Flask?

FastAPI provides async-first architecture essential for handling concurrent API requests, built-in OpenAPI documentation, and Pydantic validation that catches tax calculation errors at the boundary. The 500+ routes need automatic documentation that stays in sync with code.

Why Hash-Chain Ledger?

Auditors require proof that records weren't tampered with. Each transaction includes a SHA-256 hash of the previous transaction, creating an immutable chain. Any modification breaks the chain, providing cryptographic evidence of integrity.

Why Benford's Law?

Fraudulent financial data rarely follows natural digit distribution patterns. Benford's Law analysis flags transactions where first-digit frequencies deviate from expected distributions, automating what forensic auditors do manually.
