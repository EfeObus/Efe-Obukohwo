Northwind Traders Tableau Analysis

Executive Performance & Commercial Analytics Dashboard

2013 – 2015 Data Data Analyst Portfolio Project 21 Countries

Project Overview

Northwind Traders is the classic multi-table sample business dataset — seven relational tables covering customers, orders, order line items, products, categories, employees, and shippers. Rather than pre-joining the data outside the tool, this project connects all seven tables directly inside Tableau and builds a single executive dashboard that lets a board-level audience explore three years of global sales performance (2013–2015) without touching a spreadsheet.

The Business Problem

Raw transactional tables spread across customers, orders, and line items don't answer executive questions on their own. Leadership needed a single view that could answer: where is revenue concentrated, which products and categories actually move the business, how does demand shift across the calendar, and how efficient is the logistics network — all filterable by date range and top-N cohort, on demand.

Vision

Join seven relational tables natively in Tableau and surface one interactive dashboard — driven by Top N, Start Date, and End Date parameters — that turns three years of order-level data into an executive-ready read on revenue, customers, products, seasonality, and logistics.

Methodology & Technical Execution

Data Modeling

  • Relational join in Tableau: Connected categories, customers, employees, products, orders, order_details, and shippers directly, building the full order-to-product-to-customer chain natively rather than pre-flattening the data.
  • Scope: 830 orders and 2,155 order line items spanning 2013-07-04 to 2015-05-06, across 91 customer records and 21 countries.

Dashboard & Parameters

  • Built 16 worksheets covering revenue trend, order trend, customer trend, top customers, top products, category revenue and sub-category, average discount trend, average shipping cost per order, and carrier distribution.
  • Wired Top N, Start Date, and End Date parameters into the dashboard so every worksheet re-filters together — the same interaction pattern used to drill from full-period totals down to any custom date range or cohort size.

  • Customer concentration: The top 3 customers — Horst Kloss ($117.5K), Jose Pavarotti ($115.7K), and Roland Mendel ($113.2K) — generate $346.4K combined, or 25.6% of all revenue.
  • Product performance: Côte de Blaye is the single largest revenue driver at $150.0K, well ahead of Thüringer Rostbratwurst ($87.7K) and Raclette Courdavault ($76.3K).
  • Category volume: Beverages (286.5K units) and Dairy Products (251.3K units) dominate physical volume shipped — over 42% of total units moved.
  • Seasonality: Revenue and order volume peak in early Q2 (April) and late Q4 (December), consistent with holiday replenishment cycles, with a mid-year dip around May–June.
  • Logistics: Freight is split fairly evenly across three carriers, with United Package carrying the largest share (39.3% of shipments) ahead of Speedy Express (30.0%) and Federal Shipping (30.7%).

Strategic Impact & Recommendations

Key Account Management

Formalize VIP loyalty programs for the top 3 clients, who together represent over a quarter of total revenue — protecting the accounts with the highest concentration risk.

Inventory Seasonality Smoothing

Adjust inventory buffers ahead of the recurring May–June demand dip while staying ready for the April and Q4 volume spikes.

Carrier Contract Renegotiation

Consolidate shipping volume across United Package, Speedy Express, and Federal Shipping to bring down the $78.24 average per-order freight cost.

Category Expansion

Cross-sell premium products like Côte de Blaye into the highest-volume categories — Beverages and Dairy Products — to lift margin without adding new logistics complexity.
