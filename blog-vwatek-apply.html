Building VwaTek Apply: Kotlin Multiplatform for Smarter Job Hunting

March 2026 8 min read Efe Obukohwo
Kotlin Compose Multiplatform Gemini AI Mobile Development Career Tech

Job hunting is broken. Hours spent guessing ATS keywords, writing generic cover letters, and walking into interviews unprepared. For the Canadian market—with NOC codes, LMIA considerations, and regional salary data—it's worse.

So I built something different.

What It Does

VwaTek Apply is a career suite running natively on iOS, Android, and Web from a single Kotlin codebase. It transforms your job hunt from a manual grind into an automated, data-driven strategy.

Resume Intelligence

ATS scoring, keyword gap analysis, version control, AI rewriting tailored to specific job descriptions using the X-Y-Z impact framework.

Document Generation

Context-aware cover letters, multi-version management, professional PDF export with 4 template styles.

Interview Prep

Mock interviews with a skeptical AI recruiter, STAR method coaching, and real-time feedback on answers.

Canadian Market Data

Salary percentiles by NOC code, negotiation strategies, LinkedIn profile optimization.

The Technical Stack

One codebase. Three platforms. Full PIPEDA compliance with Canadian data residency.

Core Features Deep Dive

Resume Review & ATS Optimization

The resume intelligence module compares your resume against specific job descriptions to provide a compatibility percentage with detailed breakdown. It identifies critical hard skills, soft skills, and industry-specific terms missing from your profile.

  • Match Scoring: Real-time compatibility analysis against job descriptions
  • Keyword Gap Analysis: Identifies missing industry-specific terms
  • ATS Formatting Analysis: Checks structure, length, and keyword density
  • X-Y-Z Impact Framework: Transforms bullets using "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]"
  • Grammar & Tone Polishing: AI-powered analysis of passive voice and weak verbs

AI Interview Preparation

Engage in realistic mock interviews with an AI acting as a skeptical recruiter. The system provides STAR method coaching to structure your experiences into Situation, Task, Action, and Result format.

🎯 Feedback Loop

Input drafted answers and receive AI coaching on tone, clarity, and keyword inclusion. The tough recruiter simulation helps you prepare for challenging questions before the real interview.

Canadian Market Intelligence

Built specifically for the Canadian job market, VwaTek Apply includes:

  • Salary Intelligence: Canadian salary data with percentile analysis and market trends
  • NOC Code Matching: Automatic mapping to National Occupational Classification codes
  • Negotiation Coach: AI-powered coaching with personalized strategies
  • Job Bank Integration: Direct connection to Canada Job Bank listings
  • Bilingual Support: Full English and French language support

Security Features

  • On-Device Security: SQLDelight with encryption ensures personal work history stays private
  • Cross-Device Sync: Automatic sync with conflict resolution
  • Offline Support: Work offline with automatic sync when connection restored
  • Real-time Streaming: View AI suggestions word-by-word for responsive UX

What I Learned

Kotlin Multiplatform works. The promise of shared business logic across iOS, Android, and Web is real. The domain layer, data repositories, and ViewModels live in commonMain while platform-specific implementations handle native APIs.

Streaming AI responses dramatically improves UX. Instead of waiting for complete responses, users see AI suggestions word-by-word, creating a more engaging and responsive experience.

Encrypted storage on mobile breaks in edge cases. SQLDelight encryption works great in simulators but presents edge cases on real devices—particularly around keychain access during app updates and background sync. Test on real devices early and often.
